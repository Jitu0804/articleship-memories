A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Colleague, A Special Friend ❤️

Henali ❤️

Dear Henali ❤️

Henali, you joined just 10 days before me, so I guess I should ask — Kem chhe Senior? 😂😂

You are Jain, but when it comes to bakchodi, you are completely Non-Jain! 😂😂 I know you don't eat non-veg, but when it comes to talking and making fun, you leave absolutely nothing behind! 😂😂

Honestly, my bond with you has become really special. In the beginning, we didn't talk that much, but slowly, as time passed, our bakchodi kept increasing 😂😂 and our bonding became better and better. And one thing I can definitely say — you have a very good heart. ❤️

You got quite a few IPOs, but somehow the party never came! 😂😂 It's okay, one day you'll give it... I am still waiting! 😂😂 Although, yes, you did take me for ice cream once, so I won't completely blame you. 😂😂

But honestly, talking to you and doing bakchodi with you is always fun. We have also spent quite a lot of time together during audits, and thank you for helping me whenever I didn't know something or needed help. ❤️

And towards the end, our leg-pulling became next level 😂😂. We both tease each other so much, but Henali, no matter how hard you try, I am never going to use any bad words for you! 😂😂😂

You often tease me by saying that "We are just colleagues." So let me make one thing very clear — you were never just a colleague to me. ❤️ Our friendship is much more than that, and I genuinely hope we stay connected even after Articleship, especially during our study period.

I still remember our PCIPL incident 😂😂. While coming back, I hadn't brought my bike, so we had to somehow manage three people on one bike. But you both thought I would take the bike, and since it had started raining, you ordered a raincoat thinking that you two would go together and I would take another way. 😂😂

And then, after the raincoat arrived, I said, "Arey, I am also coming with you!" 😂😂😂 That three-person bike ride became one of those memories that I will never forget. ❤️

Then we had so much fun at the National Conference too. And yes, how can I forget Taksh Galaxy — you went alone and played the games, and didn't even take me with you! 🙂🙂🙂 It's okay, I'll remember that! 😂😂

And then came our Pavagadh trip and Sindhrot trip — so many plans, so much masti, and so many memories. ❤️ And honestly, these plans were successfully possible only because of you, Henali! You took the initiative, made things happen, and gave us memories that we will always remember.

Thank you for being such a wonderful friend, for all the help, all the masti, all the trips, all the jokes and all the memories. ❤️ And if during these 2 years I ever said something that hurt you, or made you feel bad, then dil se sorry, Senior. ❤️

And whenever I teased you by taking someone else's name or pulled your leg, it was always just for fun. 🤌😂 Thank you and Sorry, Senior. 🤌❤️

You are genuinely an amazing person, Henali. Stay the same, keep smiling, keep doing bakchodi, and most importantly — don't disappear after Articleship! 😂❤️

— Jitu Suthar
