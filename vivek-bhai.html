The GST Guide I Always Wanted to Learn From ❤️

Vivek Bhai ❤️

Dear Vivek Bhai,

Vivek Bhai, you handle all the GST-related work in our office, and you are honestly one of the calmest people I have met. You mostly keep yourself focused on your work, but whenever I have asked you any doubt, you have always explained it to me very nicely, with proper examples, so that I could actually understand it.

I have personally liked GST from the beginning, so from Day 1 I used to tell you, “Vivek Bhai, mane GST sikhvadjo hhh, and mane kaam pan aapjo.” 😂 And you would always say, “Are jaroor, tu free thaine keh, to tane mota mota client nu kaam sikhvadi dav.”

But honestly, I never really became free from my Audit work. 😂 So even though I couldn't work with you as much as I wanted to, you did show me some work and taught me things in the beginning.

Still, there is one wish that I have — I genuinely want to learn GST from you someday. ❤️

I have a lot of respect for you, not just because of your knowledge, but also because of your nature. You are a very good and calm person.

And it wasn't only office work. Sometimes I would even call you regarding some personal client-related work to ask for information, and you would always explain everything to me properly and patiently.

There is also one recent memory that I definitely have to mention. 😂 A few days ago, you and I had a debate about one section, and the result came right there — I lost! 😂😂

And then you jokingly said, “Are nah, aapto party!” 😂😂

But Vivek Bhai, even though I lost that debate, I will definitely give you a party one day! 😂❤️

Vivek Bhai, thank you so much for always helping me, explaining my doubts, and supporting me whenever I needed your help.

And if during these two years I have made any mistake, said something wrong, or unintentionally hurt you in any way, I am genuinely sorry from my heart. 🙏

Thank you so much, Vivek Bhai. ❤️

And yes… one day, I will definitely learn GST properly from you! 😂❤️

— Jitu Suthar
