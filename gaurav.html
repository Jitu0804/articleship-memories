Thank you, brother, for all the masti, support, learning and this brother-like bonding. ❤️ Our brotherhood will always remain the same, and just because my Articleship is over doesn't mean our relationship is over. 💯

Let's always stay in touch, keep doing masti, and keep teaching each other new things. 🤝❤️
