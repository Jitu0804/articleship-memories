A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Principal, A Guide Who Always Pushed Me Forward ❤️

CA Rangoli Ma'am ❤️

Dear Rangoli Ma’am,

Ma’am, you are my Principal because I am registered under you as an Article, but for me, your role in my Articleship has always been much more than just being my Principal. You have always guided me, supported me, and made sure that I was learning and growing throughout these two years.

Since you are an Income Tax expert, whenever we had any doubt related to Income Tax, we would come to you and ask. And there is one reply of yours that I think I will remember forever. 😂😂

“Section vachine aavo.” 😂

You always believed that if we learned to read the language of the Bare Act, we would not get stuck anywhere. That is why you always preferred that we first read the Income Tax section from the Bare Act, understand it, and try to interpret it ourselves. And if we had understood something incorrectly, you would then explain it to us very nicely.

That way of teaching has helped me a lot because you didn't just give us the answer — you wanted us to learn how to find the answer ourselves.

Another thing I really want to thank you for is approving my Scholarship. Because of that scholarship, we were able to receive financial assistance from the Institute on a quarterly basis. Ma’am, thank you for helping me with that. It genuinely helped us a lot.

There were also many times when some discussion was going on among the Articles and no one else was around. Even then, you would often be there with us. You supported us enough to sit with us, understand what we were discussing, explain things to us, and even tell us whether our decision was right or wrong.

During Audit season, there were also many moments when you used to take everyone's remand. 😂 Sometimes you would point out mistakes and tell us things that I still remember.

One sentence I will always remember is:

“Capital Gain joine nakhjo.” 😂

And another one:

“Aa set nu conceptual kone karyu?” 😂

And of course:

“Error-free joine karjo yaar, bav bhulo made chhe.” 😂

During Audit season, when many people were making mistakes in these points, you would sometimes sit everyone together and explain everything. But what I really appreciated was that if someone felt bad about something you said, you would personally meet them later and calmly explain it to them.

That showed me that behind all the strictness, you genuinely cared about whether we were learning and improving.

You also taught me Direct Tax in CA Intermediate, and you were my Principal at that time too. So from CA Intermediate to these two years of Articleship, I have had the opportunity to learn from you for quite a long time.

And Ma’am, whenever I had any doubt, no matter how many times I asked you the same thing, you always explained it to me. That patience is something I will always be thankful for.

Another thing I really appreciate is that you always personally asked us about our syllabus — how much we had completed, how much was remaining, and whether we were studying properly along with our work.

Whenever we were wasting time or doing time pass, you would remind us:

“Study karo, bilkul time waste nahi karo.”

You always made sure that along with Articleship work, we were also giving proper time to our studies. That balance between work and studies is something you always reminded us about.

Ma’am, these two years with you were genuinely a great experience. I have learned many things from you, not only about Income Tax or Audit, but also about how to approach a problem, how to understand things properly, and how to keep learning instead of simply looking for answers.

Thank you, Ma’am, for teaching me, guiding me, supporting me, correcting me, and always pushing me towards studying and improving. ❤️

And if during these two years I have made any mistake, said something wrong, or unintentionally hurt you because of anything, I am genuinely sorry from my heart. 🙏

I will always be thankful that you were my Principal and one of the people who guided me throughout this Articleship journey.

Thank you for everything, Rangoli Ma’am. ❤️

— Jitu Suthar
