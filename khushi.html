A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Colleague, A Friend ❤️

Khushi ❤️

Dear Khushi ❤️

Khushi, you are genuinely such a nice person. You are only 10 days senior to me 😂, but honestly, you have always been one of the most helpful people around me. You are the kind of person who can even leave your own work for a moment just to help someone else, and I have personally experienced that many times. ❤️

Since both of us are from the TP 13 area, I know that even after Articleship, we are definitely going to meet and stay in touch. 😎😂 And from the beginning, I have always felt very comfortable with you because both of us came from a Gujarati-medium background, so we understand each other's struggles very quickly. 😂 Sometimes without even saying much, you already understand that, "Aa ne kaik kaam hase, kari aapvu joie." 😂❤️

During these 2 years, whenever I needed help, you always helped me — many times even before I properly asked. You have always had that helping nature, and that is something I genuinely appreciate about you. We have also spent a lot of time together on different audits, and we have made so many good memories during those days. ❤️

And of course, our masti was always there. 😂😂 Being around you was never boring, and I am really going to miss all those random conversations and fun moments.

One serious advice before you leave — please ride your scooty a little slowly! 😂😂 You drive it like you are already late for the next audit. Please thodu speed ochhu karje, Khushi! 😂😂

I also want to say one thing from my heart. Because of that WhatsApp group message, you also ended up crying, and honestly, that day was one of the worst days of my Articleship. I never wanted to hurt you or anyone, and I am genuinely sorry, Khushi. ❤️

One more memory I will always remember is the day when we went to the garden after lunch. We were supposed to finish lunch break at 2, but we came back around 2:45, and then Ma’am called us and gave us the lecture 😂🤌. At that time, it was just another fun moment, but now it has become one of those memories that I will always remember.

And the National Conference was another level of fun! 😂 We had such a great time there, and those moments are definitely going to stay with me for a long time.

Khushi, thank you for always being so helpful, understanding and supportive. ❤️ I am really happy that during these 2 years, I got a friend like you.

And if I have ever said or done anything that hurt you or made you feel bad, I am genuinely sorry. ❤️

10 days senior hoy toh senior rehje, pan have office pachhi pan friendship aavi j rehvi joie! 😂❤️

TP 13 ma toh malta j rahishu. 😎🤝

— Jitu Suthar
