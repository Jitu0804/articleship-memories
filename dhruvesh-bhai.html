A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Colleague, A Brother & A Memory I’ll Always Carry ❤️

Dhruvesh Bhai ❤️

Dear Dhruvesh Bhai,

You are like my elder brother. ❤️ Most of my days during these two years were spent sitting beside you. In the beginning, I had a lot of respect for you, but I wasn't very comfortable with doing too much masti with you. But as time passed, I got very comfortable with you, and in the current stage, we do so much bakchodi together. 😂😂 I also pull your leg a lot, but you never take anything badly, and that is one of the things I really like about you. Your nature is genuinely one number. ❤️

You have a huge interest in movies and series, and now you have even taken a Crunchyroll subscription again to watch anime. 😂 You somehow know about almost every song and every movie. And yes, you never taught me TDS. 😂😂 But honestly, I will still put that blame completely on you — “TDS nu kaam toh sikhvadyu j nathi!” 😂 Because somehow I was never free from my own work. But apart from work, I got to learn many real-life things from you.

You also know quite a lot about the Lord, and many times during lunch breaks, you used to tell us stories and talk about the Lord. Sometimes you even told us real ghost cases, and honestly, I used to be very interested in listening to those stories. 😂 I also really enjoyed listening to your talks about the Lord, so I used to listen to you very carefully.

You are also a person who takes very good care of your things, especially your phone. 😂 You have a real interest in branded phones, shoes, clothes and all those things. And on top of that, you are a gym boy, so the body is also maintained! 😂😂

Many times, while joking around, you used to tell us, “When you all leave the office, you will remember that there was one Dhruvesh Bhai.” 🥹🥹 And then you would call yourself a “Ghelphadiya manas” 😂😂😂😂 and we would laugh so much.

With you, we never had to think before talking. We could even speak freely and sometimes say things that were a little too much 😂, but you always took them in a fun way and replied with the same energy.

And sometimes you would say such powerful things that we could not stop laughing. 😂 Like when you were laughing a lot, you would say, “Aaje toh bav hasu chhu, bapor pachhi Ma’am kaam sathe besadse toh radavse mane!” 😂😂

Many times you even gave me cash and helped me with GPay, so Dhruvesh Bhai, now please find another bank! 😂😂 And when we used to sit side by side for lunch, I hope sometimes you will remember me when you sit down to eat. ❤️

One time you even gave us a party and we went to have Limbu Shikanji, and that was also absolutely amazing! 😂❤️ Thank you for that memory.

And Bhai, I am genuinely going to miss you. It feels strange that I have to leave after spending so much time with such a jordaar manas. But we are brothers, and a brother never really leaves another brother. ❤️ Our relationship will always remain the same.

Keep sharing all your new stories with me. 😂

You once told me, “Jitu, tara farewell par koi rade ke na rade, hu radis.” 😂😂

Dhruvesh Bhai, I'm waiting yaarrr! 😂😂😂😂

And yes, your love for your phone is something else. 😂 You take care of your phone more than your own life. You even buy ₹1000–₹1200 covers for it. 😂 And one day, when your phone fell from my hands, you didn't even say anything. You just said, “Are kai nai, ema su...” 😂❤️

That moment genuinely meant a lot to me — because for a moment, it felt like you gave more importance to me and our relationship than even your beloved phone. 😂❤️ And honestly, I loved that.

And there is one more story that you told us recently which made me laugh so much. 😂😂 You had gone to your in-laws' place in a four-wheeler, and your father-in-law praised you in front of your father, saying that his son-in-law had learned to drive a car. 😂

And your father said, “Are nah nah, haju reverse leta nathi aavtu!” 🤣🤣🤣🤣

Then you told us, “Are pan tamne koi e puchhyu ke reverse leta aavde chhe ke nai?” 😂😂😂😂😂😂

And then when your mother-in-law asked your father-in-law whether the son-in-law had learned to drive, he simply said, “Haan, pan reverse leta nai aavdtu!” 😂😂😂

Bhai, honestly, there are so many stories and moments with you that I could keep writing forever. But I have to keep this message limited, otherwise you will get bored reading it. 😂😂

So let's just say — thank you for being such a wonderful brother, such a fun person, and such an important part of my two years. ❤️

Thank you for everything, and sorry for anything I may have done or said during these two years that hurt you.

I will genuinely miss you, Bhai. ❤️

And yes, Bhabhi ne mari yaad aapjo! 💯😎🤙

— Jitu Suthar
