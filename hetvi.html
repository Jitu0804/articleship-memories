A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Colleague, A Friend ❤️

Hetvi ❤️

Dear Hetvi ❤️

It honestly feels a little strange to write this message because when I joined this office, I never thought that I would get such a good junior and such a beautiful bond during my Articleship. ❤️

You are genuinely a very good person, and our bonding was never just about work. We had so much masti, bakchodi, random conversations and fun moments together that these two years would not have been the same without you. ❤️

Whenever there was work, you were always ready to help. I can genuinely say that I got one of the best teams during my Articleship — a team that never stepped back from supporting me or getting the work done. And you were an important part of that team. ❤️

We shared so many small moments in the office which may have felt normal at that time, but now I know that these are the moments I am going to miss the most. The random talks, laughing together, helping each other with work, and all the small masti we used to do in between work — everything has become a memory now.

And one thing I want to make very clear — our relationship is not limited to the office. ❤️ Office khatam thai gayu etle apno rishto khatam thai jaay evo bilkul nathi. I genuinely want us to stay connected even after Articleship, and I hope we continue meeting, talking and making new memories together.

Thank you for doing your work, supporting me whenever I needed it, and for making these two years so much more enjoyable. ❤️ I may have been your senior at work, but honestly, I also learned many things from you.

And yes, I know I have pulled your leg, irritated you, joked around and sometimes probably crossed the line in masti 😂😂. So if I have ever said or done anything that hurt you or made you feel bad, I am genuinely sorry. My intention was never to hurt you.

Bas ek j vaat — aa bonding office sudhi limited nathi rakhvani. 😂❤️

Keep growing, keep learning, keep doing well, and whenever you learn something new, don't forget your senior! 😂😂

Thank you for being such an important part of my Articleship journey. ❤️

See you outside the office too — because this is not a goodbye, it's just a change of workplace. 🤝❤️

— Jitu Suthar
