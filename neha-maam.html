A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Teacher, You Were Like a Mother During My Articleship ❤️

CA Neha Ma'am ❤️

Dear Neha Ma’am,

When I think about these two years of Articleship, there are some people whose presence made this journey special, and you are definitely one of those people. But Ma’am, for me, you were not just a teacher or a senior. From the very beginning, you took care of me in a way that always felt like a mother.

You are a very kind and emotional person, and from day one, you have always taken so much care of me. You have listened to my things, understood me, and supported me. Even when I had something personal to share, you always listened to me patiently and understood what I was trying to say.

Since you handle Audit-related work in our firm, there is probably no small or big thing related to Audit that I haven't asked you about during these two years. 😄 Sometimes, if I didn't understand a logic or a particular thing, I would ask you again and again — even three or four times. But every single time, you explained it to me patiently until I finally understood it. You never showed anger or irritation, and you always made sure that I actually understood the concept instead of simply moving on.

That patience of yours is something I will always remember.

And Ma’am, one thing I can never forget is the way you supported me even when it came to my personal work. Whenever I had to leave the office for some personal reason, whether someone else was with you or not, you were always supportive. You would give me permission and never made me feel uncomfortable about asking for leave.

In these two years, I honestly don't remember even a single day when you and I had any argument or serious disagreement.

But then came that WhatsApp group message.

A few days ago, I was just in a masti and joking mood, and we friends had posted a selfie in the office WhatsApp group with a message saying that if we worked till late, it would be nice if we got an incentive. It was just a joke from our side, and there was absolutely no bad intention behind it. But I know that the message hurt you, and for that, Ma’am, I genuinely want to say sorry from my heart. 🙏

When you called us to your cabin to discuss it, and you became emotional and cried while saying, “If you have any problem, then tell me, but please don't write something like this,” that moment honestly broke my heart.

I can genuinely say that that was the worst day of my two years of Articleship. The thought that something I had said or done had made Neha Ma’am cry is something I will never forget.

If I could go back and change one thing from my Articleship, I would definitely go back and remove that message from the group. I wish I had never posted it.

But Ma’am, what touched me even more was that you forgave all of us. ❤️ You didn't hold that incident against us, and today, our bonding is still so good. That shows what kind of person you really are.

There is one more funny thing I remember. 😄 I used to come late to the office quite often — actually, most of the time. 😂 Prachi Di would sometimes tell you, “Ma’am, you explain it to him.” But honestly, I don't even remember you ever scolding me about it. That was again your way of understanding me rather than simply getting angry with me.

From day one until today, you have always treated me so well. And honestly, however much I write about you, it will still feel less.

I also remember when I had to go for the Bhuj and Ahmedabad audits. Since I love travelling and exploring new places 😄, when I got the opportunity to go with you for the audit, you allowed me to go without hesitation. And you even told me, “Jalsa karine aavjo, memories banavjo… kaam toh thai jase.” ❤️

That sentence is something I will always remember.

Because even while thinking about work, you understood that these two years are also a time for us to experience things, travel, enjoy and make memories.

Whenever I was unwell, you would be the one asking me, “Kem office aayo? Ghare aaram karay ne.” You would ask whether I had taken my medicine, and if I wasn't feeling well, you would tell me to just go home and rest.

Those may sound like small questions, but for me, they never felt small.

They felt like the questions of a mother who genuinely cares about her child. ❤️

And Ma’am, for taking care of me like that throughout these two years, I genuinely want to say thank you from my heart.

Honestly, the way you have treated me and cared for me — in front of that, no incentive, stipend or anything else has any value for me. The care, support and understanding you gave me are much more valuable than anything else.

Thank you, Ma’am, for teaching me, patiently explaining things to me again and again, supporting me in my personal work, trusting me, taking care of me, forgiving me, and most importantly, making me feel cared for throughout these two years. ❤️

And once again, if during these two years I have made any mistake, said something wrong, or unintentionally hurt you in any way, I am genuinely sorry from my heart. 🙏

No matter where life takes me after Articleship, I will always remember the way you treated me.

You will always remain one of my favourite people from my Articleship journey, Neha Ma’am. ❤️

Thank you for everything, Ma’am. And once again, sorry and thank you for taking care of me like a mother for these two years. ❤️

— Jitu Suthar
