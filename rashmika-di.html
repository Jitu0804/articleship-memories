Dear Rashmika Di,

You are also like my sister. ❤️

You are genuinely a very good-hearted person and someone who always enjoys masti and jokes. I have really enjoyed spending time with you and having fun with you.

And I have one more special memory. During Diwali, you had arranged two bundles of ₹20 notes for me when I wasn't getting a positive response from any of the banks. 😂❤️ You helped me when I really needed it, and I genuinely remember that.

So next Diwali also, please keep some bundles ready for me! 😂😂 So that I can easily get them. ❤️

Thank you so much for always being so good to us, for all the masti, for helping us whenever we needed you, and for everything you have done for me. ❤️

And if I have ever said anything wrong or unintentionally hurt you during these two years, I am genuinely sorry from my heart. 🙏

And yes, whenever the next IPO comes, I will definitely remember you! 😂😂❤️

Thank you for everything, Di. ❤️

You will always be my sister and a very special part of my Articleship memories. 🥹❤️
