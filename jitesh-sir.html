A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Mentor, A Part of My Articleship Journey

CA Jitesh Sir ❤️

Dear Jitesh Sir,

When I think about these two years of Articleship, there are so many things that come to my mind. But one thing I will always remember is how comfortable and positive you made the office environment for us.

You are one of the calmest and most respectful people I have met. I have genuinely never seen you getting angry with us. Even when something went wrong, you always handled it calmly. Your way of talking to everyone was always respectful, and that is something I have always admired about you.

One thing I really liked about you was that you never made us feel like there was a big difference between a Partner and an Article. You always treated us with respect and made us feel like we were a part of the team. That comfort and trust meant a lot to me.

Sir, I still remember that day when we were all in the basement for some celebration. You were there with us and suddenly asked the Articles, “Kyarek Mass Bunk karo chho ke nai?” 😂 And then you pointed towards the back door and said, “Aa pachhad no darwajo ena mate j chhe.” 😂😂

That moment was so unexpected and funny. It was just a small joke, but these are exactly the kind of little moments that made our office environment so comfortable and memorable. Even today, whenever I remember that moment, I smile. 😂

You always encouraged us not to compromise with our studies. You used to tell us to read and keep learning along with our work. I really appreciated that because you never wanted us to forget that Articleship was not only about completing office work, but also about becoming better professionals.

I also remember the trust you gave us. You gave us freedom and never made us feel like someone was constantly watching over us. That trust made us feel responsible for our own work and also gave us confidence.

And Sir, one memory which is completely different from work but very close to my heart is Miransh. ❤️🙂

Whenever Miransh came to the office, he would play with me, and honestly, I really enjoyed those moments. The way he used to call my name and interact with me always made me smile. These little moments became such a sweet part of my Articleship that I know I will genuinely miss him.

I still remember when I visited your home and Miransh became emotional and didn't want me to leave. 🥹 He wanted to keep playing, and honestly, even I didn't feel like leaving. That was such a small moment, but it touched my heart. I will genuinely miss Miransh too. ❤️

These are the kinds of memories that make me realise that Articleship was never only about work for me. It was about the people, the relationships, the small jokes, the conversations and all these little moments that made these two years special.

I have also learned a lot from you just by observing the way you handle people and situations. Whether it was dealing with work pressure, clients, or simply maintaining a positive environment in the office, there are many small things that I will carry forward with me.

When I joined as an Article, I never thought that after two years I would have so many memories with the people here. But today, when I am leaving, I realise that Articleship taught me much more than just CA work.

It taught me how to work with people, how to handle different situations, how to take responsibility and, most importantly, how important good people are in making a workplace feel like home.

Thank you, Jitesh Sir, for trusting me, supporting me, motivating me to study, giving me opportunities to learn, and most importantly, for making these two years feel like much more than just Articleship.

I will genuinely miss the office, the people, the conversations, the jokes and this whole environment. ❤️

And Sir, if during these two years I ever made any mistake, said something wrong, or unintentionally hurt you in any way, I am genuinely sorry from my heart. It was never my intention to hurt you.

Thank you for everything, Sir. ❤️

The Articleship may be over, but the respect, relationships and memories will always remain with me.

— Jitu Suthar
