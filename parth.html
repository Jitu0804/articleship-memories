A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Junior, A Younger Brother ❤️

Parthu ❤️

Dear Parthu ❤️

Parthu, you are my junior, but honestly, senior-junior has never really mattered between us. You are like a younger brother to me. ❤️

You are a very calm, simple and almost Swami-type person 😂😂. But once you get comfortable with someone, then you become completely different and do some jordarrrrr masti. 😂 You speak a little slowly, but whatever you say is always nice and genuine.

We haven't spent a huge amount of time together, but whatever time we did spend together has always been really good. And I genuinely want to tell you that you are like my younger brother, and this senior-junior thing will never come between us. Even after office, we will stay connected. ❤️

And I can't forget our lunch-break games. 😂❤️ Those lunch breaks when we all used to sit together, play Scribble and then Codenames, and just enjoy our time together were genuinely fun. Sometimes we used to get so involved in the games that lunch was less about eating and more about enjoying those few moments together. 😂 I really wonder sometimes — when will we get those lunch breaks and those days back again? Those small moments have become some of the best memories of our Articleship. ❤️

And one special request, my brother — please stop playing games at 2–3 AM at night! 😂😂 Leave those games and sleep peacefully, brother. Your sleep is more important! 😂😂

We also went together for quite a few audits, and we had a lot of fun there. Those memories will always stay special. ❤️

Thank you, Parthu, for being such a simple, genuine and sweet person. And if I ever said anything or did anything during these 2 years that hurt you, then dil se sorry, brother. ❤️

Senior or junior doesn't matter — our brotherhood will always remain the same. 🤝❤️

— Jitu Suthar
