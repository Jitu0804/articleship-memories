A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Senior, A Person I Will Always Remember ❤️

CA Prakash Sir ❤️

Dear Prakash Sir,

When I think about these two years of Articleship, there are many people and many memories that come to my mind. You are definitely one of the people who made this journey special for me.

One thing I really like about you is your sense of humour. You are a very humorous person, and your jokes and way of talking always made the office environment more enjoyable. 😄

Another thing I have noticed about you is that even when there is a lot of workload, you never get angry with us because of it. You always handle things calmly, and I really respect that quality of yours.

Sir, there is one thing that I genuinely admire about you — your English. The way you speak English so fluently has always impressed me. Whenever I heard you speaking, I used to think that I also want to speak English this fluently and confidently. In a way, you inspired me to work on my English and become more confident while speaking. ❤️

There is also one small thing that I always noticed and really liked about you. In the morning, you keep the photo of your Guru and do Aarti. 🙏 I always found that habit very beautiful, and I genuinely respected that connection with your Guru and your faith.

These may look like small things, but during these two years, these are the little things that I noticed and will remember about you.

When I joined as an Article, I never thought that these two years would give me so many memories and relationships. But today, when my Articleship is over, I realise that the journey was not only about work. It was also about the people around me and the things I learned from them.

And Sir, from you, I will always remember your humour, your calm nature, your fluent English and the small things that showed your values.

Thank you, Prakash Sir, for being a part of my Articleship journey and for all the memories. ❤️

And Sir, if during these two years I ever made any mistake, said something wrong, or unintentionally hurt you in any way, I am genuinely sorry from my heart. It was never my intention to hurt you.

I will genuinely miss the office, the people, the conversations, the jokes and this whole environment. ❤️

The Articleship may be over, but the respect, memories and relationships I made here will always remain with me.

Thank you for everything, Sir. ❤️

— Jitu Suthar
