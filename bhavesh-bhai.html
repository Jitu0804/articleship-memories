A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Colleague, A Brother & A Memory I’ll Always Carry ❤️

Bhavesh Bhai ❤️

Dear Bhavesh Bhai ❤️

Bhavesh Bhai, you are our Marathi Bhai, and whenever I met you, I would always ask, “Kasa hai Bhau?” 😂❤️

You are a little serious and short-tempered sometimes — especially when someone does something wrong in the office. 😂 But with me, you always spoke very calmly and never got angry with me, and I really appreciate that.

Whenever we all used to sit late in the evening, we would order snacks and have our little snack parties, and you would always participate in them, which I really liked. 😂❤️ Many times, you would even tell us, “Are nasto mangai lo, tension nah lo!” 😂

One thing I remember about you is that you always believed that an Article's mistake can be forgiven once, but if the same mistake happens again, it will definitely appear in the office group. 😂😂 Like during the monsoon, when water used to fill up in our office basement, we would keep all the laptops on higher places. But if any Article accidentally forgot to move their laptop, you would take a photo and send it to the office group. 😂😂 Thankfully, this never happened with me! 😂

Bhau, this is just a memory I am sharing, so please don't take it the wrong way. ❤️ I am only remembering all those funny moments we had together.

And yes, you also make really good chai, Bhau, and I am definitely going to miss that! ☕❤️ I don't know about everyone else's work, but whenever I had any work, you would always help me get it done. 😂😂

You might be a little serious with others, but I always managed to do masti with you. 😂 Many times, I even used ChatGPT to translate Gujarati sentences into Marathi so that I could talk to you in Marathi. 😂😂 I used to genuinely enjoy doing that!

I even used to tell you, “Are mane Marathi sikhvado… biju kai nai toh gaado toh sikhvadi do!” 😂😂

So Bhau, you are genuinely a jordaar manas. ❤️ Thank you for always being good to me, helping me whenever I needed it, and being part of so many memories.

And if anything I ever said or did during these two years hurt you, then dil thi sorry, Bhau. ❤️

And finally…

THANK YOU in Marathi, Bhau! 🤣🤣🤣🤣❤️

— Jitu Suthar
