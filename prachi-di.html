A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

Not Just a Colleague, But My Vadodara Sister ❤️

Prachi Di ❤️

Dear Prachi Di,

Prachi Di, you are like my sister. I have my real sister in Ahmedabad, but now I can proudly say that I have one sister in Vadodara too — and that is you, Prachi Di. ❤️

From the very beginning, you have always treated me like your younger brother. Throughout these two years, you have taken so much care of me. And yes, whenever I used to come late, you would also scold me. 😂 But at the same time, you always made sure that our conveyance was given to us on time. 😂

I have done a lot of masti with you and also teased you a lot. I used to say, “Prachi Di, tame toh kasu kaam nai karta!” 😂😂 But behind all these jokes, I always knew that you have a very good and pure heart. ❤️

And as an HR, you are truly amazing! Whenever there was any festival or New Year celebration, you always came up with some amazing games and made everyone participate. Honestly, a lot of our happiness and masti during those celebrations came because of the games you arranged. ❤️

And Prachi Di, you will always be Prachi Di for me. ❤️

Sometimes, when you were emotional because of some personal reason, you also shared your feelings and बातें with me. That made me feel that our relationship was never just about being office colleagues. We have built a genuine brother-sister bond, and I really hope this bond always remains the same, even after our Articleship ends. ❤️

And whenever I used to jokingly say, “Prachi Di, paisa joie!” 😂, you would immediately say, “Are bol ne, ketla joie? Mokli dav!” 😂❤️

That itself shows how dildaar and kind-hearted you are. You are genuinely a mast person! ❤️

And how can I forget our picnic at Riparian! 😂 We had so much fun there. Whenever there was any adventure activity, Prachi Di would be like “Nooo, hu nahi aavu!” 😂 because you were scared, and we used to shake the rope even more just to scare you! 😂😂

And while going to the picnic, you made the Reel of the whole group, and honestly, so many things became even more fun and memorable because of you. ❤️

Prachi Di, you are a very soft-hearted person, and I am genuinely thankful that you have always treated me so nicely and cared for me like your younger brother.

Thank you for all the care, all the masti, all the games, all the support, and most importantly, for giving me a sister-like bond in Vadodara. ❤️

And if during these two years I have ever said something wrong, teased you too much, or hurt you in any way, I am genuinely sorry from my heart. 🙏

Thank you so much, Prachi Di, for being my sister in Vadodara, for always caring for me, supporting me, and making these two years so special. ❤️

No matter how much time passes or where life takes us, this brother-sister bond will always remain special to me. ❤️

And yes, Prachi Di — you will always have a special place in my Articleship memories. 🥹❤️

Always stay the same — caring, soft-hearted, dildaar and full of masti! 😂❤️

Thank you for everything, Di. ❤️

— Jitu Suthar
