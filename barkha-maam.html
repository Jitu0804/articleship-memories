A PERSONAL MESSAGE ❤️

More Than a Teacher, One of the Most Fun-Loving People in My Articleship ❤️

CA Barkha Ma'am ❤️

Dear Barkha Ma’am,

When I think about these two years of Articleship, there are many people and memories that come to my mind, but your presence has a very special place in those memories.

Barkha Ma’am, you are truly one of the most comedy-loving and cheerful people I have ever seen. Honestly, I don't think there is anyone else in our office as hasmukh as you. 😄 Whenever you sit with us and work, somehow the work itself becomes more enjoyable because of your one-liner jokes. Your sense of humour is just too good, and the way you make everyone laugh is something I will genuinely miss. 😂❤️

Whenever I had any doubt related to TDS, you always explained it to me very nicely. You never showed anger or irritation while explaining anything to me. Even today, I can honestly say that I have never seen you getting angry at anyone. That is something I really admire about you.

We didn't get many opportunities to sit and work for a long time in your cabin, but Ma’am, if I had been given the option to choose my sitting place anywhere in the entire office, I would definitely have chosen your cabin. 😂❤️ Because I genuinely enjoy sitting with you, talking with you and working around you.

And Ma’am, whenever there was any party, farewell or games in the basement, your presence gave a completely different energy to the whole thing. You always participated so actively and made everyone laugh. Whether it was a celebration or just some masti, your presence always made it more enjoyable.

One thing I really like about you is that even though you are a senior and a teacher to us, your nature is still just like ours — full of masti, jokes and fun. And whenever there is a chance to make fun of someone or enjoy a joke together, you are always there with us. 😂❤️ That is what makes you so different and so easy to connect with.

And Ma’am, I also want to say something from my heart about that WhatsApp group message. I am genuinely sorry that the joke we made hurt you. 🙏 There was absolutely no intention from our side to hurt you or make you feel bad. When you said, “Chalya kare, aavu toh, chinta nai karvani,” it showed me again how pure-hearted you really are. ❤️ The way you handled that situation without holding it against us is something I will always remember.

Honestly, Ma’am, you are a very pure-hearted person. I have never seen you getting angry with anyone, and even after being our senior, you always remained connected with us through the same masti, jokes and fun.

Thank you so much, Ma’am, for teaching me, explaining my TDS doubts, always supporting me and making the office environment so much more fun. ❤️

These two years would definitely not have been the same without your jokes, your laughter, your participation in every celebration and all those small moments with you.

And Ma’am, if during these two years I have ever made any mistake, said something wrong, or unintentionally hurt you in any way, I am genuinely sorry from my heart. 🙏❤️

I will genuinely miss the office, the people, the masti, the jokes, the celebrations and especially people like you who made this place feel so special.

The Articleship may be over, but the memories, respect and relationships I made here will always remain with me. ❤️

Thank you for everything, Barkha Ma’am. ❤️

— Jitu Suthar
